# Project: Japan Global & ZuCity Marketing Operating System & Execution Engine

## Architecture
The Japan Global & ZuCity Marketing Operating System provides a comprehensive, production-ready strategic positioning framework, content engine, campaign execution apparatus, and performance measurement operations suite. It bridges Balaji Srinivasan's *The Network State* (TNS) paradigm with the authentic physical reality of rural Japanese regional revitalization (Komoro / Nagano Prefecture).

## Interface Contracts
### M1 (Brand Architecture) ↔ M2, M3, M4, M5
- All downstream documentation must reference entity names, parentage, and maturity statuses strictly matching M1 Identity Cards.
- Jargon Quarantine: High-level Network State theory and crypto-economic terms are restricted to `/network` and investor surfaces; public consumer copy must use people-and-place-first language.

### M2 (Strategic Narrative) ↔ M3, M4
- Narrative angles and value props in M3 content pillars and M4 campaign copy must align with the 3 Audience Playbooks (Builders, Nagano Locals/Gov, Investors/Partners).
- Local Japanese messaging must use respectful, non-extractive terminology (地方創生, 空き家再生, 関係人口) and standard business Japanese (*Keigo*).

### M3 (Content Engine) & M4 (Campaigns) ↔ M5 (Measurement & Review)
- All published assets must be verifiable against the 5-Point Pre-Publication Review Rubric in M5.
- All campaign conversion paths must route exclusively to verified live digital surfaces (`zucity.org/en/all`, `zucity.org/en/calendar`, JGN Discord).

### M7 (Operational Audit) ↔ M8, M9, M10
- Only verified live endpoints (`zucity.org/en/all`, `zucity.org/en/calendar`, verified Discord links) may be marked active in M8 launch pack and M10 dashboard. Any unverified/offline surfaces must be explicitly labeled `[PROPOSED]` or `[UNKNOWN]`.
- Blocker classifications in M7 directly inform the risk matrix in M10 operations dashboard.

### M8 (ZuCity Launch Pack v1) ↔ M11 (Gatekeeper Review)
- All 5 X hooks/threads, 3 IG visual briefs/scripts, and 1 community announcement must pass the 5-point binary pre-publication rubric before publication sign-off.
- Tone must be people-and-place first, strictly separating live prototype (ZuCity) from future city vision (Chokyo).

### M9 (Analogue Benchmark) ↔ M8 (Launch Pack) & M10 (Operations)
- Pricing, packaging, and positioning insights from M9 benchmark must be integrated into the seasonal offers and messaging angles in M8.
