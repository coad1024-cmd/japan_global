#### 1. Core Definition & Epistemic Scope
Spotlights the human fabric of the ecosystem: visiting AI researchers, resident open-source developers, local Nagano carpenters, master sake brewers, municipal revitalization liaisons, and founding stewards. Every post celebrates high-agency agency, craft mastery, and cross-cultural collaboration `[Web-Verified]`.

- **Primary**: Persona 1 (Global Digital Nomads & Builders) — Shows them their future peer group.
- **Secondary**: Persona 2 (Local Japanese Stakeholders) — Demonstrates respect and appreciation for local wisdom.

#### 3. Recurring Narrative Angles
- **The Reverse Diaspora Profile**: Why an elite engineer or founder left San Francisco, London, or Tokyo to build in the mountains of Komoro.
- **Master Craftsperson Spotlight**: A deep look into the lifetime dedication of local Nagano woodworkers, soba masters, or sake brewers collaborating with ZuCity.
- **The Unlikely Collaboration**: A pairing of a global technologist and a local Akiya owner solving a physical problem together.
- **Founding Steward Reflections**: Raw, authentic notes from community leaders on building high-trust culture and physical spaces.

#### 4. Raw Asset Requirements
- **Visuals**: High-resolution environmental portraits (natural light, unposed, in workspace or workshop; 3:2 landscape and 4:5 vertical).
- **Audio/Video**: 30–60 second vertical video clips of the subject speaking or working in their craft; clean microphone audio.
- **Direct Quotes**: 2–3 verbatim quotes on focus, alpine living, and community dynamics.
- **Context Metadata**: Name, background, current project, favorite local spot in Komoro.

- **Woodworking & Joinery Mastery**: Traditional Japanese wood joinery (*Kigumi*) requiring zero nails, and its modern architectural application.

- **Visuals**: Tactile macro photography (sake vats, wood grain, ceramic tea sets, steam, hands at work).
