## 1. Visual Brand Identity, Color Palette & Aesthetic Philosophy

The Instagram surface is the visual and aesthetic flagship of ZuCity and the Japan Global Network. It communicates the harmonious intersection of **ancient Japanese architectural heritage** and **cutting-edge digital focus**.

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐

├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│                                                                                                  │

│   • Mountain forests & mist    • 150yo hand-carved beams    • Natural straw fragrance & warm light│
│                                                                                                  │

│   • Volcanic rock & onsen      • Crisp alpine winter air    • Midnight focus & deep contrast     │
│                                                                                                  │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 1.1 Aesthetic Philosophy
- **Wabi-Sabi Modernism**: Celebrating the authentic patina of aged timber, natural plaster textures, and asymmetric beauty, elevated with clean modern lines and ergonomic workstations.
- **Cinematic Stillness**: Rejecting frantic, chaotic hyper-editing. Every visual asset should evoke deep calm, alpine clarity, and high-agency focus.
- **Sensory Groundedness**: Capturing tactile details—the grain of 150-year-old wood, rising onsen steam, pouring hot green tea, hand-cut soba noodles, and soft mechanical keyboard clicks.

## 2. Reel Aesthetics, Video Pacing & B-Roll Composition

Reels are our primary discovery engine on Instagram. They must capture attention within the first 3 seconds while maintaining an elevated, documentary-grade aesthetic.

```
[00:00 - 00:03: SENSORY HOOK] ──► [00:03 - 00:15: NARRATIVE TENSION] ──► [00:15 - 00:30: SENSORY PROOF] ──► [00:30 - 00:40: CONVERSION CTA]
```

### 2.1 Video Technical Specifications
- **Aspect Ratio**: 9:16 Vertical (1080 x 1920 px).
- **Frame Rate**: 4K 60fps (for smooth slow-motion b-roll) or 24fps (for cinematic documentary feel).
- **Target Duration**: 18–35 seconds (optimal for loop completion and algorithmic push).
- **Typography / Captions**: Custom clean serif or geometric sans-serif (e.g., Neue Haas Grotesk / EB Garamond); high-contrast white text with subtle shadow.

### 2.2 The 4-Part Reel Compositional Sequence

| Timestamp | Segment Name | Visual Shot Type | Audio Design | Overlay Text Hook Example |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **00:00 – 00:03** | **Sensory Visual Hook** | Macro shot of onsen steam rising, cedar beam joinery, or typing by a snow-covered shoji window. | Crisp ambient foley (water splash, timber creak, keyboard stroke). | *"What if your home office looked like this?"* |
| **00:03 – 00:15** | **The Narrative Contrast** | Rapid cut between intense deep work (dual screens, code) and peaceful alpine environment (Mount Asama, pine forest). | Atmospheric, warm lofi / neo-classical piano swell. | *"Left high-cost city noise to build in the Japanese Alps. Here's day 14."* |
| **00:15 – 00:28** | **The Lifestyle & Culture** | Communal dinner around traditional hearth (*Irori*), morning walk to local cafe, master carpenter at work. | Rich, immersive music with soft background laughter/clinking glasses. | *"Gigabit fiber, hot springs, and dinner with founders every evening."* |
| **00:28 – 00:35** | **The Clean CTA** | Wide sunset exterior of ZuCity restored estate; smooth fade to minimalist logo lockup. | Gentle music resolve. | *"Autumn residency applications now open. Link in bio."* |

## 5. Story Cadence, Daily Rhythms & Interactive Highlights

Stories maintain real-time authentic engagement and give followers an unpolished, behind-the-scenes look into daily life in Komoro.

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                                   DAILY STORY CADENCE (3–5 FRAMES)                               │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  08:30 JST (Morning Vista)    ──► Mount Asama sunrise / morning coffee in restored lounge.       │
│  12:30 JST (Local Shinshu Lunch) ──► Hand-made buckwheat soba at local 80yo family shop in Komoro│
│  15:30 JST (Deep Work / BTS)  ──► Screen capture of code / master carpenter inspecting timber.  │
│  19:00 JST (Community Dinner) ──► Family-style dinner around the hearth with interactive poll.   │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 5.1 Highlight Organization & Cover Design
Maintain 6 evergreen highlights with minimalist line-art icon covers on charcoal background:
1. **🏠 Spaces**: High-res room tours, workstation specs, and fiber speed tests.
2. **🏔️ Komoro**: Local onsens, hiking trails, cafes, and seasonal weather updates.
3. **🛠️ Renovations**: Time-lapse construction footage and before/after transformations.
4. **👥 Community**: Resident testimonials, unconference moments, and family dinners.
5. **📅 Residencies**: Upcoming cohort themes, dates, and application guides.
6. **📰 FAQ**: Transport from Tokyo (70 min Shinkansen), visa details, and packing lists.

## 6. Sound Design & Audio Selection Guidelines

Audio establishes 50% of the emotional resonance of Instagram video content:

### 6.1 Approved Audio Categories
- **Authentic Field Foley**: High-fidelity environmental audio (flowing alpine streams, crackling fireplace, temple bells at Kaikoen, rain on cedar shingles).
- **Minimalist Neo-Classical / Ambient**: Artists similar to Ryuichi Sakamoto, Nils Frahm, Max Richter, or Joe Hisaishi piano arrangements.
- **Warm Lofi Beats**: Gentle, acoustic instrumental hip-hop/lofi with zero aggressive synth drops.

### 6.2 Prohibited Audio Styles
- ❌ **FORBIDDEN**: Loud, aggressive EDM, trap beats, or jarring club music.
- ❌ **FORBIDDEN**: Cheesy "hype bro" motivational speeches with shouting voiceovers.
- ❌ **FORBIDDEN**: Overused, generic royalty-free corporate ukulele/whistle tracks.

## 7. Prohibited Instagram Tropes & Brand Guardrails

To protect the dignity of our community and the cultural heritage of Nagano, creators must strictly avoid these negative tropes:

- ❌ **The "Digital Nomad Cliché"**: Posing with a laptop on a sandy beach or balancing a laptop on an onsen ledge.
- ❌ **The "Poverty Safari / Blight Exploitation"**: Photographing decaying abandoned homes in a mocking or sensationalist manner.
- ❌ **The "Exoticizing Gaze"**: Treating Japanese locals or traditions as superficial cartoon props.
- ❌ **The "Fake Crypto Baller"**: Flaunting luxury cars, watches, or champagne bottles; ZuCity represents grounded craft and quiet mastery.
