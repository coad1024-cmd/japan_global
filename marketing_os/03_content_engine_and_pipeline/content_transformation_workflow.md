## 1. Executive Summary & Transformation Engine

The **6-Stage Content Transformation Workflow** is the core operational engine of the Japan Global Network (JGN) Marketing OS. It establishes a repeatable, high-throughput manufacturing line that converts raw, uncurated real-world occurrences in Nagano—a builder conversation over onsen tea, a structural timber renovation milestone, an open-source commit, or a community dinner—into high-converting, culturally revered, platform-native digital assets.

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                                 THE 6-STAGE TRANSFORMATION LINE                                  │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│                                                                                                  │
│   STAGE 1 ──► STAGE 2 ──► STAGE 3 ──► STAGE 4 ──► STAGE 5 ──► STAGE 6                            │
│   RAW         OBSERVE     STRATEGIC   PLATFORM    MULTI-      MEASURE &                          │
│   CAPTURE     & EXTRACT   ANGLE       FORMAT      CHANNEL     FEEDBACK                           │
│   (Field/Git) (Insight)   (Pillar)    (Native)    (Publish)   (Loop)                             │
│                                                                                                  │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 1.1 Core Operational Principles
1. **Never Create from Scratch**: Content is not invented in an air-conditioned office; it is harvested directly from verified physical and digital reality in Nagano.
2. **Zero Waste (1-to-N Multiplication)**: Every meaningful raw asset must yield at least 5–10 distinct platform-native derivative assets.
3. **Epistemic Gatekeeping**: No asset advances from draft to publication without passing strict binary truth-in-marketing checks.
4. **Non-Extractive Cultural Respect**: Authentic reverence for Japanese customs (*Wa*, *Omotenashi*, *Keigo*) is baked into every stage of the transformation line.

## 2. Stage-by-Stage Operational Instructions

---

### Stage 1: Raw Material Capture (素材収集・記録)

#### 1.1 Objective
Capture high-fidelity, uncompressed real-world data and media across physical nodes in Komoro/Nagano and digital developer surfaces.

#### 1.2 Intake Channels & Asset Formats
- **Field Audio & Interviews**: High-quality voice recordings (WAV/M4A) of resident reflections, master artisan explanations, and founder debriefs.
- **Visual Media**: RAW photo captures (3:2 landscape, 4:5 vertical) and uncompressed 4K 60fps video clips of architectural details, landscape transitions, and collaborative deep work sessions.
- **Technical & Product Logs**: GitHub pull requests, commit messages, MCP server configurations, IoT telemetry, and hackathon prototypes.
- **Spatial & Renovation Logs**: Daily construction logs from Nagano carpentry crews, structural timber inspections, and budget expenditures.
- **Community Notes**: Whiteboard sketches, Discord unconference summaries, and resident feedback cards.

#### 1.3 Step-by-Step Operator Instructions
1. **Deploy Field Capture Tools**: Coliving community managers and field liaisons maintain active capture kits (high-grade smartphones with cinematic stabilization, wireless lapel microphones, and digital notebooks).
2. **Execute Structured Logging**: After every community event, resident interview, or construction milestone, the operator logs the raw material using the **Standard Raw Asset Capture Log Template** (Section 4).
3. **Centralize to Intake Repository**: Deposit media into designated cloud storage bins organized by `YYYY-MM-DD_Location_Topic/` with strict naming conventions.

---

### Stage 2: Observation & Insight Extraction (分析・本質抽出)

#### 2.1 Objective
Filter out ambient noise, uncover the non-obvious human or technical insight, and identify the fundamental narrative tension.

#### 2.2 Analytical Framework: Extracting the Core Tension
Every compelling story contains a productive tension. The operator evaluates the raw material against four core tensions:
- **Tradition vs. Modernity**: e.g., A 150-year-old hand-hewn cedar beam supporting a modern gigabit fiber workstation.
- **Atomization vs. High-Trust Density**: e.g., Leaving lonely isolation in San Francisco to code alongside 20 aligned peers in alpine serenity.
- **Urban Decline vs. Rural Rejuvenation**: e.g., Japan’s demographic winter transformed into an opportunity for global talent integration.
- **Virtual Cloud vs. Physical Land**: e.g., Transitioning an online Discord community into a physical network archipelago node.

#### 2.3 Step-by-Step Operator Instructions
1. **Transcribe & Summarize**: Run raw audio through automated transcription; highlight the top 3 verbatim quotes containing emotional or intellectual punch.
2. **Epistemic Classification**: Assign an epistemic tag (`[Confirmed Fact]`, `[Web-Verified]`, `[Inference]`, `[Open Question]`) to every factual assertion in the raw notes.
3. **Formulate the Single Sentence Thesis**: Write one clear sentence summarizing the core insight (e.g., *"Living in a high-trust alpine village removes 90% of the cognitive overhead that slows down deep technical building in major metropolitan hubs."*).

---

### Stage 3: Strategic Angle & Persona Selection (戦略的角度・対象選定)

#### 3.1 Objective
Map the extracted insight to one of the 10 Content Pillars and tailor the narrative frame to the primary target audience.

#### 3.2 Target Persona Selection & Tone Calibration
The operator selects one primary persona and aligns the register:
- **Persona 1: Global Digital Nomads & Builders** → Register: High-agency, intellectually ambitious, practical, lifestyle-empowering.
- **Persona 2: Local Japanese Stakeholders & Municipalities** → Register: Formal *Keigo*, humble, civic-minded, protective of local heritage (*Wa*).
- **Persona 3: Ecosystem Partners & Real Estate Backers** → Register: Analytical, institutional, metric-driven, defensibility-focused.

#### 3.3 Step-by-Step Operator Instructions
1. **Select 1 Primary Pillar**: Match the insight to the most potent pillar from `content_pillars_catalog.md` (Pillars 01–10).
2. **Select Persona & Angle**: Choose the corresponding hook and value proposition from the **Master Messaging Matrix** (`marketing_os/02_strategic_narrative_and_value_props/messaging_matrix.md`).
3. **Define the Conversion Goal**: State explicitly what the reader should do after consuming this asset (e.g., Book stay on `zucity.org/en/all`, apply on `zucity.org/en/calendar`, join Discord, or contact partnership desk).

---

### Stage 4: Platform-Native Formatting & Drafting (プラットフォーム最適化)

#### 4.1 Objective
Translate the strategic angle into distinct, platform-native creative executions adhering to dedicated channel rubrics.

#### 4.2 Platform Adaptation Requirements
- **X / Twitter**: 1 hook tweet (paradox or contrast), 6–10 value-dense body tweets, 1 concluding synthesis tweet, 1 CTA tweet linking to verified URL.
- **Instagram**: 9:16 vertical Reel (3-second visual hook, fast dynamic b-roll, atmospheric audio) OR 10-slide educational carousel + bilingual caption.
- **Substack / Long-Form**: 1,500–2,500 word narrative essay, structured headings, embedded photo assets with captions, epistemic footer block.
- **Discord / Community**: Structured Markdown announcement with emoji taxonomy, bullet points, and role ping tags (`@Residency-Cohort`, `@Contributors`).

#### 4.3 Step-by-Step Operator Instructions
1. **Consult Platform Rubric**: Open the relevant rubric from `marketing_os/03_content_engine_and_pipeline/platform_operating_rubrics/`.
2. **Draft Primary Platform Asset**: Write the primary long-form or core anchor asset first.
3. **Execute Downstream Adaptations**: Draft derivative short-form assets (tweets, reels, quote cards) directly referencing the anchor copy.
4. **Dual-Register Check**: If creating bilingual assets, verify that Japanese copy uses flawless business *Keigo* and approved civic terminology (地方創生, 空き家再生, 関係人口).

---

### Stage 5: Multi-Channel Distribution & Cross-Routing (配信・誘導)

#### 5.1 Objective
Deploy content across coordinated distribution channels during optimal audience engagement windows, ensuring frictionless conversion routing.

#### 5.2 Global Timezone Staging Strategy
All content scheduling accounts for the multi-timezone global footprint:
- **JST Prime (Tokyo/Nagano)**: 08:00–10:00 JST & 18:00–21:00 JST (Local stakeholders, domestic Japan, APAC builders).
- **UTC / Europe Prime**: 16:00–19:00 JST (07:00–10:00 UTC / European morning).
- **US Prime (Eastern / Pacific)**: 21:00–01:00 JST (08:00–12:00 EDT / US morning active hours).

#### 5.3 Step-by-Step Operator Instructions
1. **Execute Pre-Publication Review Rubric**: Ensure the asset passes all 5 binary gate checks before scheduling (Section 3).
2. **Load into Scheduling Tools**: Schedule primary posts via approved social management tools (Buffer, native dashboards).
3. **Cross-Link & Amplify**: Cross-post long-form Substack essays into X threads; post highlights in Discord `#announcements`; pin high-performing threads.
4. **Route CTAs Exclusively to Live Endpoints**:
   - Stay/Coliving: `https://zucity.org/en/all`
   - Residency Events: `https://zucity.org/en/calendar`
   - Community: Official JGN Discord Invite

---

### Stage 6: Measurement, Engagement & Feedback Loop (分析・改善サイクル)

#### 6.1 Objective
Monitor real-time audience engagement, respond to inquiries, track hard business and community outcomes, and feed learnings into the next content cycle.

#### 6.2 Separating Hard Outcomes from Vanity Metrics
- **Vanity Metrics (Informational Only)**: Impressions, views, likes, raw follower counts.
- **Hard Outcome KPIs (Primary Focus)**:
  - Residency applications submitted on `zucity.org/en/calendar`.
  - Room/stay bookings completed on `zucity.org/en/all`.
  - Verified Discord community joins via custom invite links.
  - Inbound qualified Akiya property inquiries from Nagano owners.
  - High-intent partnership/investor inquiries via `/network` desk.

#### 6.3 Step-by-Step Operator Instructions
1. **First-Hour Engagement**: Monitor comments on X and Instagram during the first 60 minutes; reply promptly with thoughtful, high-signal responses.
2. **Weekly Performance Logging**: Record performance metrics in the **Weekly Marketing Review Ledger**.
3. **Feedback Synthesis**: If a post generates high engagement but zero conversions, evaluate if the CTA was too weak, misrouted, or misaligned with audience intent.
4. **Archive & Repurpose Top Performers**: Add top 10% performing posts to the **Evergreen Repurposing Vault** for re-circulation in 60–90 days.

## 3. Stage-Gate Review Checklists

An asset cannot transition from one stage to the next unless it satisfies 100% of the stage-gate checklist criteria:

```
[STAGE 1] ──(Gate 1-2)──► [STAGE 2] ──(Gate 2-3)──► [STAGE 3] ──(Gate 3-4)──► [STAGE 4] ──(Gate 4-5)──► [STAGE 5] ──(Gate 5-6)──► [STAGE 6]
```

### Gate 1 → 2: Raw Intake Clearance
- [ ] Raw asset contains uncompressed audio, RAW/4K visual media, or verified Git commits.
- [ ] Explicit consent obtained from all photographed or recorded individuals.
- [ ] Date, location (Komoro/Nagano node), and participant names accurately recorded.

### Gate 2 → 3: Epistemic & Insight Clearance
- [ ] Factual claims tagged with 4-tier epistemic badges (`[Confirmed Fact]`, `[Web-Verified]`, `[Inference]`, `[Open Question]`).
- [ ] Core insight articulates a clear tension (Tradition vs. Tech, Isolation vs. High-Trust Density).
- [ ] Zero unverified feature claims or fabricated traction metrics.

### Gate 3 → 4: Strategic Angle Clearance
- [ ] Asset mapped to exactly 1 of the 10 Content Pillars in `content_pillars_catalog.md`.
- [ ] Primary Target Persona identified and verified against `messaging_matrix.md`.
- [ ] Conversion goal explicitly identified with live URL endpoint.

### Gate 4 → 5: Pre-Publication Binary Gatekeeper Review (The 5-Point Gate)
- [ ] **Check 1 (Epistemic Truth)**: Are all product/space claims strictly verified as live?
- [ ] **Check 2 (Brand Architecture)**: Is the content routed under the correct entity handle (JGN vs ZuCity vs Chokyo)?
- [ ] **Check 3 (Tone & Lexicon Quarantine)**: Is the language culturally respectful and free of quarantined crypto jargon on public feeds?
- [ ] **Check 4 (Surface Endpoint)**: Do all links resolve to verified operational URLs (`zucity.org/en/all`, `zucity.org/en/calendar`, Discord)?
- [ ] **Check 5 (Single Actionable CTA)**: Does the post end with one clear, frictionless action?

### Gate 5 → 6: Post-Distribution Operational Verification
- [ ] Scheduled during the designated prime engagement window (JST / UTC / US).
- [ ] First-hour engagement monitoring assigned to an operator.
- [ ] Conversion tracking parameters (UTM tags) confirmed functional.

## 5. Content Incident, Retraction & Escalation Protocol

If an inaccurate, culturally insensitive, or quarantined claim is published, the Marketing Lead must execute this 4-step emergency containment protocol immediately:

```
[1. DETECT & PAUSE] ──► [2. EVALUATE & RETRACT] ──► [3. ISSUE CORRECTION] ──► [4. ROOT CAUSE AUDIT]
```

### Step 1: Detect & Pause (検知と停止)
- Immediately unpublish or pause the affected post/campaign.
- Notify the Marketing Lead and Council liaison in the dedicated `#incident-response` Discord channel.

### Step 2: Severity Evaluation (深刻度判定)
- **Severity Level 1 (Minor Technical Error)**: Broken URL, minor typo, incorrect photo credit.
  - *Action*: Update link silently or edit post; re-verify endpoint.
- **Severity Level 2 (Epistemic / Brand Violation)**: Quarantined crypto jargon on public consumer feed, conflating JGN with JGC/JPG, or premature feature claim.
  - *Action*: Delete post immediately. Rewrite draft adhering strictly to `naming_and_hierarchy_standards.md` and re-review.
- **Severity Level 3 (Cultural / Municipal Breach)**: Insensitive depiction of Japanese customs, privacy breach of local property owners, or unauthorized municipal claim.
  - *Action*: Delete post immediately. Issue a formal, humble apology in business *Keigo* drafted by a native Japanese liaison. Direct contact with affected local stakeholders within 2 hours.

### Step 3: Issue Correction & Transparent Resolution
- If the post reached wide distribution, publish a clear, humble correction adhering to epistemic truth standards.
- Never delete and pretend nothing happened if community trust was affected.

### Step 4: Root Cause Audit & SOP Update
- Document what gatekeeper check failed in the **Incident Post-Mortem Log**.
- Update the pre-publication checklist to prevent recurrence.
