## 5. Content Mix Targets & Anti-Fatigue Governance

To prevent timeline fatigue and ensure that commercial conversion efforts do not compromise brand authority, the Marketing Lead audits content volume against these strict monthly quotas:

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                                 MONTHLY CONTENT MIX DISTRIBUTION                                 │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│                                                                                                  │
│   [40%] INSPIRATIONAL & AESTHETIC ──► People (12%), Places (15%), Culture (12%)                  │
│   [30%] INTELLECTUAL & TECHNICAL  ──► Ideas (10%), Tech (8%), Stories (5%), Projects (10%)       │
│   [20%] TRANSPARENCY & COMMUNITY  ──► Progress (8%), Events (10%), Discord Commons              │
│   [10%] HARD CONVERSION CTAs      ──► Opportunities & Direct Booking Calls (10%)                 │
│                                                                                                  │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 5.1 Anti-Fatigue Guardrail Rules
- **Rule of Proximity**: Never publish two hard conversion booking posts (`PIL-09`) within 48 hours on the same channel.
- **Value-First Inversion**: Every booking or application call must be preceded by at least three value-dense, educational, or aesthetic assets.
- **Topic Rotation**: Never spotlight the same individual resident or restored property two weeks in a row.

## 7. Scheduling Tooling & Operational Infrastructure

The marketing apparatus utilizes a centralized, lightweight tooling stack:

### 7.1 Approved Tooling Stack
- **Editorial Hub & Master Calendar**: Airtable / Notion Editorial Database (Single source of truth for all drafts, raw media URLs, and gatekeeper approvals).
- **Social Scheduling & Publishing**: Buffer / Typefully (for X threads and daily micro-posts), Meta Business Suite (for Instagram Reels & Carousels).
- **Long-Form Publication**: Substack Dashboard (Drafting, layout staging, email newsletter broadcast).
- **Community Automation**: Discord Webhooks & Custom Bots (Automated broadcasts from Substack RSS into `#ecosystem-updates`).

### 7.2 Manual Gatekeeper Review Protocol
Even with automated scheduling tools, **no post is set to "Scheduled" status without human sign-off**:
1. The Content Creator sets the card status in Airtable/Notion to `Ready for Review`.
2. The Marketing Lead reviews the asset against the **5-Point Binary Gatekeeper Review Checklist**.
3. If approved, the Marketing Lead changes the status to `Approved & Scheduled` and affixes the Epistemic Status Tag.
