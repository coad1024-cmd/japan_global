### 1.1 Core Builder Pain Points & Friction in Japan
1. **The Tokyo Rental Wall**: Traditional Japanese leasing requires 2-year contracts, Japanese guarantor companies (*Hoshō-nin*), non-refundable key money (*Reikin*), and agency fees totaling 4–6 months of upfront rent.
2. **Isolation of Conventional Tourism**: Living out of a Western business hotel or Airbnb in Shinjuku provides zero community, no peer collaboration, and shallow transactional interactions.
3. **Flaky Rural Connectivity**: Most traditional Japanese ryokans and rural Airbnbs offer sub-30 Mbps Wi-Fi, low table seating unsuitable for 10-hour coding sessions, and no quiet call rooms.
4. **Visa & Administrative Confusion**: Navigating the new 6-month Japanese Digital Nomad Visa, 90-day visa waivers, and local registration without Japanese fluency is daunting.

## 3. Coliving Culture & Community Operating Norms

ZuCity is governed by the principle of **High-Trust In-Person Civility** (*The Network State*, Section 5.3.1). We cultivate a serious, respectful, and joyful builder culture.

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐

├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│                                                                                          │

│  12:30 - 13:30 │ Communal Lunch Sync (Local Soba / Ramen / Bento Runs)                   │
│  13:30 - 17:30 │ FOCUS BLOCK 2 (Deep Work, Code Reviews, Architecture Sprints)           │
│  17:30 - 19:00 │ Sunset Onsen Dip (Nakadana Onsen / Hot Spring Recovery)                 │
│  19:00 - 20:30 │ Family-Style Community Dinner (Cooked together or at local Izakaya)     │
│  20:30 - 22:00 │ Evening Lightning Demos, Unconferences, or Fireside Philosophy Chats    │
│                                                                                          │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 3.1 Community Covenants & House Rules
1. **The Deep Work Protocol**: The primary coworking sanctuary is a designated quiet zone during core daytime hours (09:00–17:00). All voice calls, podcasts, and collaborative meetings take place in acoustic booths or designated lounges.
2. **Communal Stewardship**: Residents participate in shared kitchen cleanup, recycling separation (following Komoro's strict municipal guidelines), and care for restored timber architecture.
3. **Opt-In Social Dynamics**: Zero forced socialization. Builders have full agency to retreat into deep solitude or participate in communal dinners, hackathons, and weekend excursions.
4. **Anti-Hostel Standard**: ZuCity strictly enforces quiet hours after 22:00 in all residential quarters. Party-house behavior, excessive alcohol consumption, and disrespectful noise are grounds for immediate stay cancellation.

### 5.1 Door-to-Desk Transit from Tokyo
1. **Tokyo Station → Sakudaira Station**: Board the Hokuriku Shinkansen (Asama or Kagayaki train) — *70 minutes*.
2. **Sakudaira → Komoro Station**: Transfer to the JR Koumi Line or Shinano Railway — *14 minutes* (or 12-minute taxi/ZuCity shuttle).
3. **Total Travel Time**: ~90 minutes door-to-door from central Tokyo to your mountain desk.

### 5.2 Local Navigation & Payments
- **Cash & Cashless**: Japan is rapidly cashless (PayPay, Suica/Pasmo transit cards, Apple Pay, credit cards), but rural local shrines, vegetable stands, and traditional onsen require Japanese Yen cash. ZuCity provides guidance on local ATM access.
- **Mobile Connectivity**: Recommended eSIMs (Ubigi, Airalo, Mobal) connect seamlessly to NTT Docomo and SoftBank 5G networks in Komoro.
