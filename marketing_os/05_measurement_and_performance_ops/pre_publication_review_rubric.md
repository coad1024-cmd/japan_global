## 1. Executive Summary & The Binary Gatekeeper Architecture

In an ecosystem bridging cutting-edge technology (*The Network State*, autonomous AI agents) with traditional rural Japanese communities (*Chihō Sōsei*, century-old timber craftsmanship), brand trust is our single most valuable and fragile asset. 

A single hyperbolic tweet claiming unbuilt crypto features or an insensitive post offending local Nagano elders can destroy months of diplomatic and cultural groundwork.

To guarantee flawless integrity, **no content may be published to any public or private channel** without passing through this **5-Point Binary Gatekeeper Review**:

## 2. The 5-Point Binary Verification Checklist

### Check 1: Epistemic Truth & Claims Verification `[PASS / FAIL]`
- **Objective**: Ensure that all statements regarding product capabilities, physical venues, and traction are 100% truthful.
- **Pass Criteria**:
  - [ ] Every factual claim matches the canonical project registry `[Confirmed Fact]` or live web endpoints `[Web-Verified]`.
  - [ ] Any forward-looking statement, architectural exploration, or roadmap item is explicitly labeled as an exploration or conceptual vision (*"planned," "designed to," "envisioned as"*).
  - [ ] Chokyo is strictly framed as the long-term future city vision; ZuCity is highlighted as the live physical neighborhood prototype.
  - [ ] Zero unverified claims regarding autonomous agent capabilities (RiChan), token returns, or unreleased software.
- **Immediate Veto Triggers**:
  - Claiming Chokyo is already built or accepting permanent citizens today.
  - Claiming RiChan has autonomous flight-booking or banking powers without human confirmation.
  - Stating unverified financial returns or speculative property appreciation.

---

### Check 2: Entity Routing & Identity Integrity `[PASS / FAIL]`
- **Objective**: Prevent brand confusion and preserve the distinct identity of all 8 ecosystem entities.
- **Pass Criteria**:
  - [ ] Content is scheduled from the correct official account handle (`@JapanGlobalNet` for umbrella network; `@ZuCityJapan` for physical stays/residencies).
  - [ ] Canonical naming orthography is followed exactly (e.g., *Japan Global Network*, *ZuCity*, *Chokyo*, *ZuJapan*, *Zaibots*, *RiChan*, *JGC*, *JPG*).
  - [ ] JGN (Public Network), JGC (Internal Governing Council), and JPG (On-chain Protocol) are never used interchangeably or conflated.
  - [ ] ZuCity's relationship to ZuJapan (formal parentage) and Chokyo (conceptual physical core) is accurately represented.
- **Immediate Veto Triggers**:
  - Using deprecated terms (*"Zuzalu Japan"*, *"Japan Global DAO"*, *"Chokyo DAO"*, *"JPG Token"*).
  - Publishing internal JGC council governance debates on consumer travel feeds.

---

### Check 3: Voice, Cultural Reverence & Lexicon Quarantine `[PASS / FAIL]`
- **Objective**: Protect cultural relationships with local Japanese stakeholders and enforce surface lexicon isolation.
- **Pass Criteria**:
  - [ ] On public consumer feeds (X, Instagram, Newsletters), the tone is warm, grounded, people-first, and culturally appreciative.
  - [ ] In domestic Japanese content, language adheres to formal business Japanese (*Keigo* / 敬語) and uses approved civic vocabulary (地方創生, 空き家再生, 関係人口, 地域共生).
  - [ ] Quarantined technical terms (*The Network State, tokenomics, DeFi, crypto ledger, sovereign secession*) are strictly confined to `/network` and developer/investor portals.
  - [ ] The framing of rural Japan is non-extractive, respectful of local traditions (*Wa* / 調和), and celebratory of master artisans.
- **Immediate Veto Triggers**:
  - Framing rural Japanese real estate as "cheap assets to exploit for lifestyle arbitrage."
  - Using aggressive crypto hype or meme jargon on Instagram or mainstream travel posts.
  - Any condescending or exoticizing remarks regarding Japanese culture or local elders.

---

### Check 4: Live Surface Validation & Route Integrity `[PASS / FAIL]`
- **Objective**: Ensure that every link provided to prospects resolves to an active, operational, error-free digital surface.
- **Pass Criteria**:
  - [ ] Every outbound link has been clicked and verified to load in $< 2$ seconds without 404 or SSL errors.
  - [ ] Accommodation bookings route exclusively to `https://zucity.org/en/all`.
  - [ ] Event and residency applications route exclusively to `https://zucity.org/en/calendar`.
  - [ ] Community invites route exclusively to verified live Discord channels (`https://discord.gg/japanglobal`).
  - [ ] UTM tracking parameters are correctly structured without broken query syntax.
- **Immediate Veto Triggers**:
  - Linking to `japanglobal.org/store` (under construction).
  - Linking to unverified third-party Google Forms or broken external landing pages.
  - Raw placeholder text inside links (e.g., `https://zucity.org/en/[TBD]`).

---

### Check 5: Single Actionable Call-to-Action (CTA) `[PASS / FAIL]`
- **Objective**: Eliminate decision fatigue and provide a frictionless, stage-appropriate next step for the reader.
- **Pass Criteria**:
  - [ ] The asset concludes with exactly **ONE** primary Call-to-Action.
  - [ ] The CTA action verb is direct, specific, and compelling (e.g., *"Apply for the Autumn Cohort on the live calendar →"*, *"Explore restored timber suites on our catalog →"*).
  - [ ] The CTA matches the user's psychological intent level (Discovery → Read Thesis; High Intent → Book Stay / Apply).
- **Immediate Veto Triggers**:
  - Including 3+ competing links in a single post (e.g., "Follow us, join Discord, book a room, and read our whitepaper!").
  - Vague, passive CTAs with no link (e.g., "Check it out sometime").

## 5. Veto Authority & Conflict Resolution Matrix

| Scenario / Disagreement | Authority & Decision Maker | Resolution Protocol |
|---|---|---|
| **Author claims a feature is "almost done"; wants to announce it.** | **Marketing Lead (Veto Power)** | Strict adherence to Check 1: If it is not observable on live surfaces today, it cannot be stated as fact. Rephrase as roadmap or cut entirely. |
| **Debate over whether a term is quarantined Web3 jargon.** | **Strategic Communications Lead** | When in doubt, quarantine. Public consumer feeds must remain 100% accessible to non-crypto audiences. |
| **Cultural sensitivity concern in Japanese text.** | **Local Bridge Lead (Native Japanese)** | Local Bridge Lead holds unconditional veto authority over domestic Japanese copy and municipal briefs. |
| **High-stakes architectural or governance dispute.** | **Japan Global Council (JGC)** | Escalated to Thursday Council session; held from publication until formal consensus is logged in council minutes. |
