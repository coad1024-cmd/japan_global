### Section 7: Exhaustive FAQ Compendium
- **Q: What visa do I need to attend?**
  - *A*: Passport holders from 68+ countries (including US, UK, EU, Canada, Singapore, Australia) enter Japan visa-free on a 90-day tourist landing permission. Japan also offers a dedicated 6-month Digital Nomad Visa for qualifying remote earners.
- **Q: How do I get to ZuCity from Tokyo?**
  - *A*: Take the Hokuriku Shinkansen from Tokyo Station to Sakudaira Station (70 minutes, ¥6,000). Our dedicated shuttle meets residents at Sakudaira and drives 12 minutes directly to the ZuCity Komoro campus.
- **Q: Are meals included?**
  - *A*: Yes. Four communal dinners per week are prepared by local private chefs featuring seasonal Shinshu organic produce, soba, and local dishes. Residents also have 24/7 access to fully stocked chef kitchens and local grocery markets.
- **Q: What is the refund policy?**
  - *A*: Deposits are 100% refundable if cancellation is made at least 30 days prior to Day 0. Within 30 days, deposits may be transferred to future ZuCity stay credits on `https://zucity.org/en/all`.
