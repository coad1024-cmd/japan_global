### Section 4: Global Events & Luma Calendar Integration
- **Live Event Feed**:
  1. **Weekly Global Town Hall & AMA**: Every Thursday at 13:00 UTC / 22:00 JST on Discord Stage. Featuring live progress updates from Komoro and guest technical presentations.
  2. **Bi-Weekly Show-Your-Work Lightning Demos**: Every other Tuesday at 12:00 UTC / 21:00 JST. 5-minute builder demos of software shipped from ZuCity.
