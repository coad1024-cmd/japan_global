## 4. UTM Parameter Conventions & Attribution Tracking

To accurately track campaign performance across multi-channel distribution without polluting brand links, all campaign URLs must follow standard UTM formatting:

### 4.1 UTM Parameter Standard
`https://zucity.org/en/[endpoint]?utm_source=[platform]&utm_medium=[format]&utm_campaign=[campaign_id]&utm_content=[variant]`

- **utm_source**: `x`, `instagram`, `substack`, `discord`, `luma`, `partner`
- **utm_medium**: `social_thread`, `reel`, `story`, `newsletter_body`, `announcement`, `direct`
- **utm_campaign**:
  - `residency_autumn_2026`
  - `stays_year_round_komoro`
  - `community_onboarding_q4`
  - `corporate_offsite_b2b`
- **utm_content**: `hook_v1_paradox`, `hook_v2_burnrate`, `video_architectural`, `speedtest_proof`

## 5. Fallback & Graceful Degradation SOP

If a marketing link encounters a broken endpoint or third-party service outage:
1. **Automated Server Redirect**: Cloudflare edge rules instantly route any `404 Not Found` path under `japanglobal.org/*` or `zucity.org/*` to the primary live catalog at `https://zucity.org/en/all`.
2. **Discord Fallback**: If an external application form experiences downtime, the CTA is temporarily updated across social profiles to direct applicants to the `#admissions-desk` channel in the official JGN Discord.
3. **Escalation Protocol**: Marketing Lead immediately notifies the Infrastructure Working Group in Discord `#ops-alerts` for resolution within 60 minutes.
