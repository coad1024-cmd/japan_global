## 1. Campaign Objective & Core Philosophy

The **ZuCity Popup City Residency** is a 2-to-4 week intensive gathering of 20–35 high-agency technologists, AI researchers, Web3 builders, architects, and creators held in the restored heritage properties of Komoro, Nagano Prefecture. 

The objective of this campaign playbook is to attract top-tier global talent, achieve a minimum 3× application oversubscription rate, ensure deep cultural integration with local Japanese community stakeholders, and convert visiting residents into long-term network participants and ecosystem contributors.

## 2. Phase-by-Phase Campaign Execution Blueprint

### Phase 1: Teaser & Theme Announcement (Weeks -6 to -5)
- **Primary Goal**: Generate high-intent anticipation and establish the intellectual theme of the residency.
- **Target Channels**: X (Twitter) Main, JGN Discord (`#announcements`), Substack Newsletter.
- **Key Actions**:
  1. Finalize residency theme (e.g., *Autonomous Agents & Physical World Interfaces*, *Decentralized Urbanism & Heritage Architecture*, or *Alpine AI & Deep Work Sprint*).
  2. Confirm physical node capacity (e.g., 24 private suites across ZuCity Node 01 and Node 02 in Komoro).
  3. Publish a long-form conceptual thread on X detailing why this theme matters and why rural Nagano is the optimal setting.
  4. Launch a pre-registration waitlist ping on Discord to alert early-stage builders 48 hours before public applications open.
- **Deliverables**: 1 Long-form X Thesis Thread, 1 Substack Teaser Memo, 1 Discord Pre-Announcement.

### Phase 2: Application Window Open (Weeks -4 to -3)
- **Primary Goal**: Drive qualified builder applications to `https://zucity.org/en/calendar`.
- **Target Channels**: X, Instagram (Reels/Stories), Discord, Builder Communities (Farcaster, Hacker News, niche AI/Web3 groups).
- **Key Actions**:
  1. Flip application form live on `https://zucity.org/en/calendar`.
  2. Deploy high-converting headline campaign across social channels focusing on gigabit fiber, restored 150-year-old timber architecture, and daily onsen access.
  3. Host a 45-minute live Twitter Space / Discord Stage AMA: "Inside the ZuCity Alpine Residency: Theme, Living Spaces, and Who Should Apply."
  4. Send Application Broadcast Email #1 to the master newsletter subscriber list.
- **Application Window Duration**: Exactly 14 calendar days.

### Phase 3: Selection & Cohort Curation (Weeks -2 to -1)
- **Primary Goal**: Evaluate submissions, conduct screening interviews, issue acceptance letters, and secure 50% booking deposits.
- **Target Channels**: Direct Email, Private Discord Cohort Channel, Stripe/Crypto Payment Gateway.
- **Key Actions**:
  1. Review applications against the 4-part **Resident Qualification Rubric** (Proof-of-Work, Thematic Fit, Community Culture, Autonomous Focus).
  2. Issue Rolling Acceptance Letters via automated email containing private payment link for deposit collection.
  3. Enforce a 72-hour deposit confirmation window; reallocate unclaimed slots to waitlisted applicants.
  4. Once cohort reaches 100% capacity, publicly announce "Applications Closed — 3.4× Oversubscribed" on social feeds to reinforce prestige and network value.

### Phase 4: Pre-Arrival Onboarding & Logistics (Week -1 to Day 0)
- **Primary Goal**: Eliminate travel friction, ensure cultural preparedness, and set community expectations.
- **Target Channels**: Dedicated Discord Role (`@Autumn-2026-Cohort`), Email Nurture Sequence, Notion/Handbook Portal.
- **Key Actions**:
  1. Grant accepted residents the private Discord cohort role and introduce them in `#cohort-introductions`.
  2. Send Automated Onboarding Emails #2, #3, and #4 (Vision, Cultural Etiquette, Transit Guide).
  3. Host mandatory "Welcome to Nagano Pre-Arrival Sync" (covering Shinkansen route from Tokyo Station to Sakudaira Station, local Komoro connector, trash sorting, onsen etiquette, and quiet hours).
  4. Coordinate dietary requirements and arrival timestamps with local catering and Komoro property management.

### Phase 5: Live Residency Amplification (Weeks 1 to 4)
- **Primary Goal**: Document the authentic daily experience, amplify resident shipping velocity, and showcase local community harmony.
- **Target Channels**: Instagram (Daily Stories & Cinematic Reels), X (Daily Build Dispatches), Substack (Weekly Roundups).
- **Key Actions**:
  1. **Day 1**: Welcome dinner at traditional Soba restaurant in Komoro; orientation and living covenant signing.
  2. **Weeks 1–3**: Daily rhythm (Morning quiet deep work → Afternoon unconference/walks → Evening hot spring recovery → Communal family dinners).
  3. **Weekly Dispatches**: Publish 2 builder spotlight threads per week on X and 3 high-aesthetic Instagram Reels highlighting architecture, cuisine, and coding sprints.
  4. **Community Civic Day (Week 2)**: Residents participate in a local Komoro volunteer activity (e.g., community garden weeding, English conversation circle with local high school students, or joint craft workshop with local artisans).
  5. **Demo Day (Final Weekend)**: Public livestream and local showcase where residents present shipped code, research papers, and creative artifacts.

### Phase 6: Post-Residency Alumni Recap & Conversion (Weeks +1 to +2)
- **Primary Goal**: Immortalize cohort outcomes, distribute on-chain credentials, and convert residents into recurring stay bookers and network ambassadors.
- **Target Channels**: Substack Documentary Feature, X Mega-Recap Thread, Alumni Discord Channel (`#alumni-network`).
- **Key Actions**:
  1. Publish comprehensive Demo Day Recap featuring GitHub links, project screenshots, and resident testimonials.
  2. Distribute digital residency completion credentials / POAPs to all participants.
  3. Issue Post-Stay Survey (measuring NPS, internet satisfaction, space quality, and community value).
  4. Provide all alumni with exclusive early-bird booking access for year-round stays via `https://zucity.org/en/all` and invitations to join JGN working groups.

## 3. Resident Qualification Rubric & Scoring Model

To maintain exceptional intellectual density and social harmony, every residency applicant is evaluated on a 100-point scale across 4 core criteria:

| Evaluation Criteria | Weight | What We Look For | Disqualifying Red Flags |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Proof-of-Work & Shipping Track Record** | 35% | Active GitHub repos, published research, shipped venture products, architectural portfolios, or demonstrated mastery in a hard domain. | Generic resume fluff, no public code/work samples, purely theoretical claims without execution history. |
| **2. Thematic Alignment & Stated Goal** | 25% | A specific, achievable project or research goal to build during the residency that directly aligns with the cohort thesis. | Vague intentions ("I just want to travel and relax"), no clear project focus. |
| **3. Cultural Fit & Community Contribution** | 25% | High emotional intelligence, enthusiasm for communal dinners, willingness to give a workshop/talk, respect for Japanese customs (*Wa*). | Entitled attitude, history of toxic community behavior, extractive "digital nomad party" mindset. |
| **4. Autonomous Self-Sufficiency** | 15% | Professional remote autonomy, comfortable in quiet rural environments, self-directed daily schedule. | Expectation of 5-star full-service hotel pampering; inability to function in peaceful alpine isolation. |

### Scoring & Decision Thresholds:
- **85–100 Points**: **Tier 1 Immediate Acceptance** (Issue acceptance letter within 48 hours).
- **70–84 Points**: **Tier 2 Interview / Waitlist** (15-minute video screening call with Community Lead).
- **< 70 Points**: **Polite Rejection** (Encourage applying for open community stays or public events).

## 4. Financial Model, Deposit Collection & Logistics

### 4.1 Transparent Pricing & Accommodation Tiers
- **Private Deluxe Suite (Restored Kominka Master Bedroom)**: $2,800 – $3,400 USD / 4-Week Cohort (Includes private bath, dedicated workstation, high-speed fiber, daily onsen pass, communal dinners 4 nights/week).
- **Private Standard Suite (Traditional Tatami Room)**: $2,100 – $2,500 USD / 4-Week Cohort (Includes private bedroom, shared bath, dedicated coworking desk, daily onsen pass, communal dinners).
- **Shared Double Room (Builder Pair)**: $1,400 – $1,700 USD / Person / 4-Week Cohort (Ideal for co-founders or collaborative developer pairs).

### 4.2 Deposit Collection & Cancellation Policy
1. **50% Non-Refundable Deposit**: Due within 72 hours of acceptance to secure room allocation.
2. **Remaining 50% Balance**: Due 14 days prior to Day 0.
3. **Cancellation Terms**:
   - Cancellations > 30 days before start: 50% deposit credited toward future ZuCity stays on `zucity.org/en/all`.
   - Cancellations < 30 days before start: Deposit forfeited to protect fixed estate leasing and catering commitments.

## 5. Resident Handbook Outline (Operational Master Index)

Every confirmed resident receives access to the digital **ZuCity Resident Handbook**, containing:
1. **Welcome & Philosophy**: The story of ZuCity, Komoro, and the Chokyo future-city vision.
2. **The Shinshu Living Covenant**: Quiet hours (22:00–08:00), kitchen cleanup rules, shoes-off policy (*Genkan*), and waste separation protocols (Burnable, Plastics, Cans, Glass, PET).
3. **Workspace Operations**: Connecting to the 1 Gbps fiber Wi-Fi 6 mesh, Zoom booth booking rules, monitor and keyboard checkout.
4. **Onsen Protocol**: How to visit Nakadana and local onsen (pre-wash etiquette, towel etiquette, hydration, tattoo policies).
5. **Local Navigation & Commerce**: Walking map of Komoro, grocery stores (Tsuruya supermarket), local cafes, Sakagura sake breweries, hospital/pharmacy guide.
6. **Emergency & Medical Protocol**: English-speaking emergency contacts, seismic safety rules, evacuation points in Komoro.

## 6. Campaign Risk Matrix & Mitigation Strategies

| **Application Volume Below Target (< 2× oversubscribed)** | Low | High | Activate co-marketing with aligned Web3/AI newsletters; host emergency Twitter Space with prominent alumni; extend window by 5 days. |
| **Last-Minute Resident Cancellations** | Medium | Medium | Maintain active pre-vetted waitlist of 10+ Tier 2 applicants ready to deploy within 7 days. |

| **Internet Service Disruption** | Extremely Low | Critical | Dual-redundant network architecture: NTT 1 Gbps primary fiber + Starlink Business satellite failover mesh on-site. |
