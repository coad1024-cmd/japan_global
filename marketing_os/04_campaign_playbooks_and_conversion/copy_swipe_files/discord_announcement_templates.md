## 1. Broadcast Formatting Rules & Channel Etiquette

To maintain community trust and prevent notification fatigue:
1. **Role Ping Discipline**: Use `@everyone` only for critical ecosystem milestones (max 1x per month). Use `@here` for time-sensitive live town halls. Use targeted cohort/interest roles (`@Builder`, `@Resident`, `@Nomad`) for domain announcements.
2. **Visual Structure**: Always start with a distinct emoji header, followed by a bold 1-sentence summary, a clean bulleted breakdown of facts, and a single high-contrast CTA link.
3. **Live Surface Verification**: Every link must resolve to a tested, live operational URL (`zucity.org/en/calendar`, `zucity.org/en/all`, or Discord stage).
