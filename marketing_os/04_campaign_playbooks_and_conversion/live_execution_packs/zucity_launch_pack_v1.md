### 1.2 Target Personas & Core Value Propositions
1. **Global Tech Nomads & Protocol Builders**:
   - *Pain Point*: Burnout in expensive, chaotic Western cities; shallow transient popup cities with zero permanence.
   - *Value Prop*: High-speed deep work (1 Gbps NTT fiber), natural volcanic hot springs (Nakadana Onsen), affordable living, and high-density builder camaraderie beneath Mount Asama.
2. **Remote Engineering Teams & Startup Founders**:
   - *Pain Point*: Ineffective offsites and fragmented team focus.
   - *Value Prop*: Dedicated campus buyouts, 120-person commons, ergonomic focus desks, fresh local gastronomy, and zero-distraction alpine sprints.
3. **Japanese Diaspora & Regional Revitalization Advocates**:
   - *Pain Point*: Rural decay, abandoned heritage architecture, and extractive tourism models.
   - *Value Prop*: Respectful, non-extractive revitalization (*Chihō Sōsei* / 地方創生) creating an active "Relationship Population" (*Kankei Jinkō* / 関係人口) that supports local artisans and farmers.

### 1.3 Epistemic Boundaries & Truth-in-Marketing Standards
To ensure 100% truth-in-marketing and protect brand integrity, all execution materials strictly observe the following rules:
- **ZuCity vs. Chokyo**: ZuCity is highlighted as the *concrete, operating, live physical prototype* in Komoro. Chokyo (長京) is strictly quarantined as the *20-year future city vision* for long-form philosophical essays.
- **RiChan & Zaibots**: RiChan is accurately represented as a *hybrid human-assisted concierge* ($4 USDC credits on the catalog); Zaibots is positioned as *open-source developer tooling*. No claims of unverified fully autonomous AI agents are permitted.
- **Booking Flow Truth**: Physical bedrooms require review and application via `https://zucity.org/en/apply/zucity-contributor` rather than instant uncurated checkout.
