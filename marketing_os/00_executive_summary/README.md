## 2. Ecosystem Portfolio Architecture & Structural Hierarchy

The ecosystem is structured as a coordinated portfolio of 8 distinct entities. Each entity possesses a defined parentage, priority ranking, maturity status, and dedicated operational boundary.

### 2.1 Formal Registry Hierarchy

Based on the canonical project registry snapshot, the formal structural tree is:

```

├── Chokyo (長京) [Parent: JGN | Priority: 10 | Flagship Future-City Project]

    └── ZuCity [Parent: ZuJapan | Priority: 8 | Physical Neighborhood Prototype]
```

### 2.2 Governance & Protocol Layers (Isolated from Network Brand)

- **JGC (Japan Global Council)**: Internal governing council and operational system layer. Distinct from JGN and JPG; never used interchangeably.
- **JPG (JapanGlobalSystem)**: Base on-chain protocol and cryptographic ledger layer (Canonical Symbol: `JPG`). Distinct from JGN and JGC.

### 2.3 The Critical Structural Nuance: Formal Parentage vs. Conceptual Alignment

A foundational requirement of the Marketing OS is maintaining the exact distinction between formal parentage and conceptual alignment:
- **Formal Registry Parentage**: `ZuCity` is formally registered under `ZuJapan` (`JGN` → `ZuJapan` → `ZuCity`).
- **Conceptual Narrative Alignment**: `ZuCity` serves as the living physical prototype, testbed, and operational core of `Chokyo` (the long-term physical megacity vision).
- **Rule for Operators**: Operators must never collapse or "fix" this distinction. When explaining corporate/organizational structure, cite the formal registry. When explaining long-term urban evolution and residency roadmaps, explain that ZuCity is the living embryonic proof point of the Chokyo vision.

## 3. Epistemic Integrity & Truth-in-Marketing Standard

Every marketing asset, claim, and campaign within the ecosystem is governed by a strict 4-tier epistemic classification framework:

| Epistemic Tier | Definition | Operational Rule for Marketing Operators |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Confirmed Fact** `[Confirmed Fact]` | Explicitly established in the canonical Japan Global project registry (snapshot 2026-08-25). | Can be stated authoritatively across all approved public channels. |
| **2. Web-Verified Fact** `[Web-Verified]` | Observable on live, public digital surfaces (`zucity.org`, ETHGlobal project showcases as of 2026-08-30). | External evidence; valid for operational conversion, but must not overwrite canonical registry definitions. |
| **3. Inference** `[Inference]` | A logical extension or hypothesis regarding product roadmap, market behavior, or architectural evolution. | Must be explicitly framed as exploration, proposal, or conceptual roadmap; never stated as present reality. |
| **4. Open Question** `[Open Question]` | Architectural, economic, technical, or operational parameters not yet established. | Must be researched, flagged, and clarified internally; prohibited from public promotional claims. |

### 3.1 Live Surface Truthfulness

Conversion funnels and public calls-to-action (CTAs) must route traffic **exclusively** to verified live surfaces:
- **Live Surfaces (High Confidence)**:
  - `https://zucity.org/en/all` (Accommodation, experience, and local goods catalog)
  - `https://zucity.org/en/calendar` (Events, popup city residencies, stays)
  - ZuCity Concierge Agent Credits (catalog items for visitor services)
  - JGN Official Discord Community Channels (`/join-discord`)
  - JGN `/network` Portal (Long-form network state thesis and investor research)
- **Surfaces Under Construction / Active Development**:
  - `japanglobal.org` unified web app and centralized e-commerce storefront.
  - Standalone RiChan production UI.
  - Dedicated standalone ZuJapan public web portal.
  - JapanGlobalSystem (JPG) mainnet protocol deployment.
- **Future Vision**:

8. **Feedback Learning**: Continuously iterate content and campaign strategies based on hard conversion data rather than superficial vanity impressions.

## 6. Operator Quickstart: The 11-Step Marketing Workflow Protocol

Whenever developing a campaign, publishing a content series, or announcing a milestone, the Marketing Lead must execute the following 11-step protocol:

```
[1. DISCOVER] ──► [2. RESEARCH] ──► [3. DEFINE OBJECTIVE] ──► [4. IDENTIFY AUDIENCE]
                                                                      │
                                                                      ▼
[8. PUBLISH]  ◄── [7. GATE REVIEW] ◄── [6. CREATE] ◄── [5. FIND ANGLE]
     │
     ▼
[9. DISTRIBUTE] ──► [10. MEASURE] ──► [11. LEARN & ITERATE]
```

### Phase A: Strategic Framing
1. **DISCOVER**: Harvest raw ecosystem assets (builder interview, architectural renovation photo, community dinner, Github commit, local craft workshop).
2. **RESEARCH**: Verify the canonical facts against `japan_global_context_for_agy_v0.1.md` and current live surfaces (`zucity.org/en/all`, `zucity.org/en/calendar`).
3. **DEFINE OBJECTIVE**: Specify the desired outcome (Awareness, Consideration, Residency Application, Accommodation Booking, Discord Onboarding).
4. **IDENTIFY AUDIENCE**: Select the primary target persona (Global Nomads/Builders, Local Japanese Stakeholders, or Ecosystem Investors/Partners).
5. **FIND ANGLE**: Map the raw material to one of the 10 Content Pillars (People, Places, Projects, Progress, Culture, Events, Ideas, Tech, Opportunities, Stories) and select the authentic narrative tension.

### Phase B: Production & Quality Gate
6. **CREATE**: Produce platform-native assets (X thread, Instagram Reel script, Substack deep dive, Discord announcement) adhering to platform rubrics.
7. **GATE REVIEW**: Pass the draft through the **5-Point Binary Pre-Publication Review Rubric**:
   - *Check 1 (Epistemic Truth)*: Are all product claims strictly verified as live? (No unverified features).
   - *Check 2 (Brand Routing)*: Is the asset assigned to the correct entity handle? (No JGN/JGC/JPG conflation).
   - *Check 3 (Voice & Tone)*: Is the register culturally respectful, non-extractive, and free of quarantined crypto jargon on public surfaces?
   - *Check 4 (Surface Validity)*: Do all links point to operational URLs (`zucity.org/en/all`, `zucity.org/en/calendar`, Discord)?
   - *Check 5 (Single Actionable CTA)*: Is there a clear, stage-appropriate call-to-action?

### Phase C: Distribution & Compounding
8. **PUBLISH**: Schedule and deploy via approved distribution tooling (Buffer, native platforms).
9. **DISTRIBUTE**: Execute the 1-to-N repurposing cascade across complementary ecosystem channels.
10. **MEASURE**: Track primary outcome KPIs (applications, bookings, qualified joins) rather than relying exclusively on vanity impressions.
11. **LEARN & ITERATE**: Feed audience insights and conversion performance back into the next editorial cycle.
