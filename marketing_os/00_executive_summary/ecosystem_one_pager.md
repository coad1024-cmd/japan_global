## 3. The 3 Core Tension Syntheses

### 3.1 Synthesis 1: Formal Registry Parentage vs. Conceptual Alignment

```
   FORMAL REGISTRY TREE                    CONCEPTUAL LIVING EVOLUTION
   JGN                                     Chokyo (Macro Long-Term Vision)
   └── ZuJapan                                 ▲
       └── ZuCity                              │ (Grows & Scales Into)
                                           ZuCity (Micro Concrete Prototype)
```
- **The Tension**: Formal documentation places ZuCity under ZuJapan, while strategic roadmap documents describe ZuCity as the core of Chokyo.
- **The Resolution**: Treat `ZuJapan` as the formal corporate/organizational legal bridge holding the ZuCity operational node, while `Chokyo` is the multi-decade civilizational masterplan that ZuCity physically prototypes today.

### 3.2 Synthesis 2: High-Tech Frontier Nomadism vs. Local Japanese Stewardship

- **The Tension**: Global digital nomads value high-speed internet, rapid iteration, and global liquidity; rural Japanese communities value social harmony (*Wa* / 和), quiet consensus (*Nemawashi* / 根回し), and cultural continuity.
- **The Resolution**: Implement a **Dual-Register Narrative Architecture**:
  - *Global Register*: Focus on deep work sanctuaries, fiber internet, peer hacker houses, and network archipelago nodes.
  - *Domestic Japanese Register*: Focus on respectful regional revitalization (地方創生), vacant home restoration (空き家再生), related population creation (関係人口), and honoring traditional artisans (伝統文化の継承).

### 3.3 Synthesis 3: Mainstream People-First Copy vs. Quarantined `/network` Thesis

- **The Tension**: Network State theory and crypto-economic mechanisms attract visionary tech founders and investors, but alienate mainstream travelers, local governments, and traditional craftspeople.
- **The Resolution**: All high-theory Network State concepts, tokenomics, smart contract architectures, and crypto-governance terms are strictly quarantined to `japanglobal.org/network` and technical developer docs. Public social feeds (Instagram, general X, local press) use warm, people-and-place-first language.

## 5. Operator Cheat Sheet: The 5-Point Pre-Publication Gate

Before publishing any tweet, article, reel, or announcement, execute this binary gate:

1. **Epistemic Truth Check**: Is every product capability verified as live today? (`YES` → proceed | `NO` → label as "In Development" or "Vision").
2. **Brand Routing Check**: Is this published from the exact right entity account? (`JGN` for public network, `ZuCity` for local stays/events, `Chokyo` for vision essays).
3. **Voice & Tone Check**: Is the copy warm, culturally respectful, non-extractive, and free of quarantined crypto jargon on public surfaces?
4. **Surface Validity Check**: Does every link route exclusively to live endpoints (`zucity.org/en/all`, `zucity.org/en/calendar`, Discord)?
5. **Actionable CTA Check**: Is there a single, stage-appropriate call to action?
