## 2. Canonical Role & Ecosystem Function

### 2.1 What It Is
**Japan Global Network (JGN)** is the overarching public network and umbrella brand connecting Japanese and global innovators, cultural creators, diaspora communities, founders, and Japanophiles worldwide. It serves as the open top-of-funnel discovery hub for everything happening across the ecosystem: member spotlights, community gatherings, cultural commerce, physical residencies, and architectural regeneration projects.

### 2.2 What It Is Not
- JGN is **NOT** the internal governance council (which is **JGC**).
- JGN is **NOT** the on-chain base protocol or token layer (which is **JPG**).
- JGN is **NOT** a single physical coliving property (which is **ZuCity**).
- JGN is **NOT** a conventional corporate conglomerate or private equity holding company.

---

## 3. Target Personas & Stakeholder Mapping

### 3.1 Primary Personas
1. **Global Japanese Diaspora & Tech Innovators**: Japanese engineers, designers, and entrepreneurs living abroad (Silicon Valley, London, Singapore) seeking meaningful connection back to Japan's technological and cultural renaissance.
2. **Global Builders & Japanophiles**: International software developers, AI researchers, Web3 builders, and artists who love Japanese culture and seek deep, productive engagement with Japan beyond tourist cliches.
3. **Cultural Artisans & Creative Stewards**: Japanese master craftsmen, architects, tea masters, and sake brewers seeking global visibility and direct, fair-trade commerce channels without predatory middleman extraction.

## 4. Strategic Value Propositions & Elevator Pitches

### 4.1 1-Sentence Pitch
> "Japan Global Network is the worldwide public community and cultural-economic network connecting creators, builders, and innovators to the living renaissance of Japan."

### 4.2 1-Paragraph Positioning
> Japan Global Network (JGN) unites a global community of builders, diaspora leaders, and cultural enthusiasts who believe Japan’s greatest civilizational assets—craftsmanship, high-trust social cohesion, and architectural beauty—can be revitalized through decentralized frontier technology. As the public umbrella for regional physical nodes like ZuCity and long-term future city visions like Chokyo, JGN offers an open home for collaboration, global gatherings, cultural commerce, and transformative physical residencies.

### 4.3 3-Minute Comprehensive Thesis
For decades, global engagement with Japan has been polarized between two extremes: superficial mass tourism that strains local infrastructure without building deep relationships, and rigid corporate hierarchies that deter international talent. Japan Global Network pioneers a third path: a high-trust, voluntary network of creators, engineers, and cultural stewards who co-invest their time, talent, and capital into revitalizing Japan from the ground up. 

By anchoring global digital alignment into physical neighborhood prototypes (ZuCity in Komoro, Nagano) and championing a multi-decade future city vision (Chokyo), JGN demonstrates how modern digital nomads and local Japanese communities can collaborate harmoniously. Through JGN, members discover cutting-edge residency cohorts, access traditional artisanal works, participate in intellectual discourse, and contribute directly to reversing rural depopulation.

## 8. Anti-Positioning Rules & Explicit Guardrails

1. **NEVER Conflate with JGC or JPG**: Do not use "Japan Global Network", "Japan Global Council", and "JapanGlobalSystem" interchangeably. JGN is the public network; JGC is internal governance; JPG is the on-chain protocol.
2. **NO Extractive Corporate Framing**: Never describe JGN as an offshore tax shelter, a real estate speculation fund, or a mechanism for cheap foreign lifestyle arbitrage.
3. **NO Blood-and-Soil Nationalism**: Japanese cultural pride must always be framed civically and culturally, celebrating open international appreciation, diaspora inclusion, and global partnership.
4. **NO Premature Product Claims**: Do not advertise the `japanglobal.org` e-commerce store as operational until checkout and fulfillment are fully deployed and verified.
