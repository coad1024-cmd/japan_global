## 2. Canonical Role & Ecosystem Function

### 2.1 What It Is
**RiChan** is the specialized tourism and hospitality concierge agent developed under the Zaibots platform. Its canonical role is to guide visitors, digital nomads, and residency participants through their travel, accommodation, and cultural experiences in Japan, converting visitor demand into network revenue and directing economic patronage toward network-affiliated stays (ZuCity), local Japanese craft workshops, traditional restaurants, and regional hot springs (*Onsen*).

### 2.2 What It Is Not
- RiChan is **NOT** a human concierge or physical tour guide; it is an AI agent concept.
- RiChan is **NOT** a standalone, venture-backed consumer app competing with TripAdvisor or Expedia.
- RiChan is **NOT** an unverified, all-knowing omniscient bot that autonomously books unconfirmed third-party bullet train tickets without human-in-the-loop confirmation.
- RiChan is **NOT** to be definitively claimed as the live interactive chat widget on `zucity.org` without explicit technical backend verification.

## 3. Target Personas & Stakeholder Mapping

### 3.1 Primary Personas
1. **International Visitors & First-Time Japan Travelers**: Remote workers and popup city attendees arriving in Japan who need curated, English-accessible guidance on navigating regional transit (Shinkansen to Sakudaira/Komoro), local dining etiquette, and onsen customs.
2. **ZuCity Residency Participants & Coliving Guests**: Long-stay builders seeking tailored local recommendations (quiet coffee shops with Wi-Fi, mountain hiking routes, nearby coworking desks, weekend pottery classes).
3. **Local Nagano Hospitality Merchants & Artisans**: Soba restaurants, sake breweries, and craft studios in Komoro that gain high-value, respectful international customers without needing English-speaking staff.

## 4. Strategic Value Propositions & Elevator Pitches

### 4.1 1-Sentence Pitch
> "RiChan is the AI-powered hospitality concierge agent under Zaibots that seamlessly guides visitors to authentic cultural experiences and network stays in rural Japan."

### 4.2 1-Paragraph Positioning
> Navigating rural Japan as an international builder or traveler can be intimidating due to language barriers and hidden local gems. RiChan acts as an intelligent, culturally fluent concierge agent operating on Zaibots infrastructure. By understanding visitor preferences, RiChan curates customized itineraries, coordinates local services, and routes visitor spend directly to community-owned accommodations like ZuCity and trusted local artisans in Nagano.

### 4.3 3-Minute Comprehensive Thesis
Mainstream tourism in Japan is suffering from hyper-congestion in Tokyo, Kyoto, and Osaka, while rural gems like Komoro remain undiscovered by international visitors due to linguistic barriers and fragmented local information. Standard travel aggregators push generic tourist traps and take 20%+ commissions from local businesses.

RiChan reimagines hospitality for the Network State era. Conceived as a specialized agent running on the Zaibots platform, RiChan provides personalized, conversational travel planning tailored specifically to the needs of digital knowledge workers and cultural travelers. 

Whether a guest needs a quiet tatami room for deep work, directions to a 300-year-old cedar-barrel miso brewery, or assistance reserving an *onsen* stay, RiChan bridges the gap. By integrating with ZuCity Concierge Credits and local merchant rails, RiChan ensures that tourism spending directly enriches the local Nagano economy while providing travelers with an unforgettable, friction-free Japanese journey.

## 7. Voice, Tone & Messaging Rules

- **Default Register**: Helpful, charming, culturally knowledgeable, hospitable (*Omotenashi*), concise, and friendly.
- **AI Persona Transparency**: Must always be introduced as an intelligent AI concierge agent. Never pretend RiChan is a real human employee.
- **Cultural Guidance**: Communicates local Japanese customs with grace and clarity (e.g. onsen tattoo etiquette, footwear removal, quiet train norms, trash separation).

---

## 8. Anti-Positioning Rules & Explicit Guardrails

1. **NO "Autonomous Booking" Over-Promises**: Never claim that RiChan can automatically book flight tickets, issue official JR Rail Passes, or execute off-platform payments without human confirmation.
2. **NO Human Impersonation**: Never refer to RiChan as a "local Japanese woman" or "our human staff member." It is an AI agent.
3. **NO Definite Claim of Existing UI Without Backend Check**: Do not state that RiChan is definitely the specific live chatbot on `zucity.org` until technical integration is explicitly confirmed. Reference "ZuCity Concierge Services" or "RiChan Beta Concept."
