# Epistemic Standards, Naming Conventions & Lexicon Quarantine Rules

## 1. The 4-Tier Epistemic Taxonomy Framework

The foundational law of the Japan Global Marketing Operating System is **Truth-in-Marketing**. We reject the crypto-industry habit of dressing up early-stage hypotheses as established facts. Every claim, capability, and narrative asset must be explicitly categorized according to our 4-tier epistemic taxonomy:

```
┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                           4-TIER EPISTEMIC TAXONOMY                               │
├───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│                                                                                   │
│  [Confirmed Fact]  ──► Canonical truth in project registry snapshot (2026-08-25)  │
│  [Web-Verified]    ──► Publicly visible on live web surfaces (2026-08-30)         │
│  [Inference]       ──► Logical extension or strategic proposal (Must be labeled)  │
│  [Open Question]   ──► Unresolved architectural parameter (Internal research only)│
│                                                                                   │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 1.1 Taxonomy Definitions & Governance Rules

#### 1. Confirmed Fact `[Confirmed Fact]`
- **Definition**: Explicitly established in the canonical Japan Global project registry / marketing knowledge base (snapshot dated 2026-08-25).
- **Rule**: Can be stated authoritatively in all public messaging, executive summaries, and brand identity documents.
- **Examples**:
  - JGN is the public umbrella brand (`Parent: None`).
  - ZuCity is formally registered under ZuJapan (`Priority: 8`).
  - Chokyo is the flagship future-city vision in Nagano (`Priority: 10`).
  - JGN, JGC, and JPG are distinct and never interchangeable.

#### 2. Web-Verified Fact `[Web-Verified]`
- **Definition**: Observable and confirmed on public, live digital endpoints as of the latest web audit (2026-08-30).
- **Rule**: Serves as external evidence of live operational reality. Can be used for immediate conversion CTAs, but must never silently overwrite or contradict canonical registry definitions.
- **Examples**:
  - `zucity.org/en/all` is live with accommodation, experience, and artisan catalog items.
  - `zucity.org/en/calendar` is live with popup city residencies and event schedules.
  - "Concierge Agent Credits" are listed on the ZuCity catalog.
  - ETHGlobal showcase displays "AIEN by Zaibots" (agentic DeFi & MCP skills layer).

#### 3. Inference `[Inference]`
- **Definition**: A logical deduction, product hypothesis, or architectural exploration regarding what a system could become.
- **Rule**: **NEVER** present an inference as an established fact. Must always be framed using forward-looking language (*"planned," "exploring," "designed to," "envisioned as"*).
- **Examples**:
  - Zaibots will eventually host specialized agents for property management, event ticketing, and local translation.
  - ZuCity will expand from Komoro into a multi-node campus across Sakudaira and Karuizawa.
  - JPG will utilize zero-knowledge proofs for privacy-preserving residency attestations.

#### 4. Open Question / Unverified `[Open Question]`
- **Definition**: Architectural, economic, legal, or technical parameters not yet specified in canonical sources.
- **Rule**: Prohibited from external public marketing claims. Must be tracked internally by the Marketing Lead and Council until resolved.
- **Examples**:
  - The exact smart contract Layer-2 rollup deployment for JPG.
  - The specific tokenomic inflation/gas fee model for JPG.
  - Whether the current chat widget on `zucity.org` is fully wired to the RiChan autonomous LLM backend.

## 3. The `/network` Lexicon Quarantine Architecture

Balaji Srinivasan's *The Network State* theory and frontier crypto-economics provide immense intellectual depth for founders, investors, and protocol researchers. However, dumping dense Web3 jargon on mainstream travelers, local Japanese municipal officials, or traditional artisans causes confusion, mistrust, and regulatory alarm.

We resolve this through a **Strict Surface Isolation Topology**:

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                             SURFACE ISOLATION TOPOLOGY                                   │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│                                                                                          │
│  SURFACE A: PUBLIC MAINSTREAM & SOCIAL      SURFACE B: LOCAL JAPANESE MUNICIPAL          │
│  (Instagram, X Main, Newsletters, Blog)     (Town Halls, Akiya Owners, Local Press)      │
│  • Register: Warm, People-First             • Register: Formal Keigo, Civic Revitalization│
│  • Vocabulary: Spaces, Craft, Community     • Vocabulary: 地方創生, 空き家再生, 関係人口 │
│                                                                                          │
│                                        ▼                                                 │
│  SURFACE C: QUARANTINED THESIS & DEV PORTALS                                             │
│  (japanglobal.org/network, Substack Deep-Dives, Technical GitHub Repositories)           │
│  • Register: Intellectual, Rigorous, Architectural                                       │
│  • Vocabulary: Network State, Ledger of Record, Consensual Governance, Smart Contracts   │
│                                                                                          │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 3.1 Lexicon Classification & Surface Permission Table

## 4. Dual-Register Japanese-English Language Guidelines

To build unshakeable trust across both international builders and domestic Japanese communities, all content production must adhere to our **Dual-Register Translation Protocols**:

### 4.1 Register 1: Global English (Nomads, Builders & Innovators)
- **Voice Attributes**: High-agency, intellectually ambitious, clear, warm, visually evocative, and community-centric.
- **Tone**: "We are gathering world-class builders to do the best work of their lives in the breathtaking mountains of Japan, pioneering the physical prototype of future human habitation."

### 4.2 Register 2: Domestic Business Japanese (Municipalities, Neighbors & Artisans)
- **Voice Attributes**: Flawless formal business Japanese (*Keigo* / 敬語), humble (*Kenson* / 謙遜), civic-minded, deeply honoring of local heritage (*Dentō* / 伝統), and consensus-oriented (*Wa* / 和).
- **Core Japanese Lexicon Table**:

## 5. Epistemic Audit Process & Content Tagging SOP

Before any written artifact, social campaign, or landing page is released into production, the Marketing Lead must execute this 4-step Epistemic Audit:

```
[1. CLAIMS AUDIT] ──► [2. LEXICON SCAN] ──► [3. SURFACE VERIFY] ──► [4. SIGN-OFF]
  Extract every        Flag any quarantined  Confirm live URL       Apply Epistemic
  factual claim        terms on public feeds  for every CTA link     Status Badge
```

1. **Step 1: Claims Extraction**: Read the draft and extract every statement regarding product features, physical spaces, token mechanics, and organizational roles.
2. **Step 2: Lexicon Scan**: Scan for quarantined terms (`tokenomics`, `secession`, `crypto`, `DeFi`, `AI takeover`). If found on a public consumer surface or local Japanese brief, immediately rewrite using approved vocabulary.
3. **Step 3: Surface Verification**: Click/inspect every outbound CTA link. If the link does not resolve to an active, verified live endpoint (`zucity.org/en/all`, `zucity.org/en/calendar`, JGN Discord), reroute to an approved live surface.
4. **Step 4: Tag & Sign-Off**: Affix the appropriate epistemic tag to internal documentation (`[Confirmed Fact]`, `[Web-Verified]`, `[Inference]`, `[Open Question]`) and certify adherence to the 5-point gatekeeper rubric.
