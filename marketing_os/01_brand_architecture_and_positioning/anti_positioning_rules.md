# Comprehensive Anti-Positioning Rules & Brand Guardrails

## 1. Core Principles of Brand Protection & Truth-in-Marketing

The Japan Global Network and ZuCity Marketing Operating System enforces a strict **Negative Positioning Framework**. In high-stakes branding, what we explicitly choose **NOT** to say is just as important as what we communicate. 

Careless marketing claims—such as exaggerating prototype maturity, dumping speculative crypto jargon on local elders, or treating traditional Japanese culture as a commercial arbitrage asset—can permanently destroy community trust, trigger regulatory backlash, and alienate world-class builders.

```
┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                          THE 6 UNIVERSAL BRAND PILLARS                            │
├───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  1. Zero Hallucination: Never market planned features as live reality.            │
│  2. Strict Entity Disambiguation: Never conflate JGN, JGC, and JPG.               │
│  3. Non-Extractive Stewardship: Reject colonial arbitrage and speculative hype.   │
│  4. Cultural Reverence: Honor Japanese civic traditions, craftsmanship, and laws. │
│  5. Lexicon Quarantine: Restrict Network State & crypto jargon to /network.       │
│  6. AI Transparency: Never misrepresent artificial intelligence as human.        │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## 2. Universal Ecosystem Guardrails (The 6 Fatal Brand Sins)

Any marketing asset violating these 6 universal rules will be immediately vetoed by the Pre-Publication Gatekeeper:

### Fatal Sin 1: Premature Vision Marketing (The Chokyo Fallacy)
- ❌ **Forbidden**: Claiming that Chokyo is already built, operational, or open as a completed megacity in Nagano.
- ✅ **Mandatory Rule**: Chokyo must always be clearly identified as a **multi-decade future-city vision and regional masterplan**. All immediate stays, coworking bookings, and popup residencies must be attributed strictly to **ZuCity in Komoro**.

### Fatal Sin 2: Tripartite Brand Conflation (The JGN / JGC / JPG Confusion)
- ❌ **Forbidden**: Using "Japan Global Network", "Japan Global Council", and "JapanGlobalSystem" interchangeably.
- ✅ **Mandatory Rule**: Maintain strict separation:
  - **JGN** = The open public network, community hub, and cultural umbrella.
  - **JGC** = The internal governance council and decision-making operating system.
  - **JPG** = The on-chain base protocol and cryptographic ledger layer (Symbol: `JPG`).

### Fatal Sin 3: Extractive Real Estate & Tourism Framing (The Colonial Trap)
- ❌ **Forbidden**: Describing rural Japan as "cheap real estate to exploit for lifestyle arbitrage," "bargain *Akiya* flipping," or "an unregulated foreign playground."
- ✅ **Mandatory Rule**: Position all physical initiatives as **mutually beneficial regional revitalization (*Chihō Sōsei*)**, architectural heritage restoration, and respectful community stewardship.

### Fatal Sin 4: AI Personification & Human Impersonation
- ❌ **Forbidden**: Describing RiChan or any Zaibots agent as a "local Japanese woman," "human travel guide," or concealing its artificial intelligence nature.
- ✅ **Mandatory Rule**: All software agents, algorithms, and automated assistants must be explicitly identified as AI tools and digital services.

### Fatal Sin 5: Jargon Pollution on Public Consumer Surfaces
- ❌ **Forbidden**: Dumping terms like *tokenomics, DeFi liquidity, validator staking, zero-knowledge rollups, or Network State secession* on Instagram, consumer travel pages, or local Japanese government decks.
- ✅ **Mandatory Rule**: Quarantine all crypto and Network State technical vocabulary exclusively to `japanglobal.org/network`, developer documentation, and specialized investor briefings.

### Fatal Sin 6: Broken Funnel & Phantom Link Routing
- ❌ **Forbidden**: Including CTAs that route users to non-existent, unverified, or under-construction URLs (e.g. non-live `japanglobal.org/shop`, unverified booking widgets).
- ✅ **Mandatory Rule**: All conversion CTAs must route **exclusively** to verified live surfaces (`https://zucity.org/en/all`, `https://zucity.org/en/calendar`, JGN Discord).

## 5. Brand Violation Remediation & Incident Response SOP

If an accidental brand violation, premature claim, or quarantined jargon leak occurs on public surfaces, the Marketing Lead must execute the following 4-step remediation protocol:

```
[1. DETECT & HALT] ──► [2. ASSESS IMPACT] ──► [3. CORRECT / RETRACT] ──► [4. ROOT CAUSE]
  Identify flagged      Evaluate severity       Issue polite revision     Update pre-pub
  content asset         & stakeholder risk      or replace live link      review rubric
```

1. **Step 1: Detect & Halt**: Immediately pause or unpublish the offending social post, email campaign, or landing page section.
2. **Step 2: Assess Severity**:
   - *Low Severity (Lexicon Leak)*: Crypto term on a public thread → Edit tweet or clarify in comments with plain language.
   - *Medium Severity (Broken CTA / Entity Misrouting)*: Dead URL or JGN/JGC confusion → Reroute link immediately to `zucity.org/en/all` and issue a corrected post.
   - *High Severity (Premature Vision / Cultural Disrespect)*: Claiming Chokyo is built or publishing culturally insensitive framing → Issue a formal correction, reaffirming that ZuCity is our live physical prototype in Komoro and reaffirming our deep commitment to local Japanese partnership.
3. **Step 3: Corrective Replacement**: Replace the offending copy with an approved asset verified against the 5-point pre-publication rubric.
4. **Step 4: Post-Mortem & Rule Update**: Document the incident in the internal marketing review log and adjust the pre-publication checklist to prevent recurrence.
