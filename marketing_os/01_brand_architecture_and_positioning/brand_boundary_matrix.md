# 8x8 Interaction & Cross-Routing Brand Boundary Matrix

## 1. Architectural Overview & Boundary Principles

The Japan Global ecosystem comprises 8 distinct entities spanning public networks, physical future cities, living neighborhood nodes, cross-border bridges, agent infrastructure platforms, consumer hospitality agents, internal governance systems, and on-chain protocol layers. 

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                                   THE 8 ECOSYSTEM ENTITIES                               │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  1. JGN (Japan Global Network)      5. Zaibots (Agent Economic Platform)                 │
│  2. Chokyo (長京 Future City)       6. RiChan (Hospitality Concierge Agent)              │
│  3. ZuCity (Physical Prototype)     7. JGC (Japan Global Council Internal OS)            │
│  4. ZuJapan (Japan ↔ World Bridge)  8. JPG (JapanGlobalSystem Protocol Layer)            │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

To prevent brand cannibalization, user confusion, regulatory friction, and epistemic hallucination, marketing operators must adhere to strict boundary rules governing how entities reference one another.

### Core Boundary Tenets
1. **Strict Tripartite Disambiguation**: `JGN` $\neq$ `JGC` $\neq$ `JPG`. JGN is the public network; JGC is internal governance; JPG is the on-chain protocol. They are never interchangeable.
2. **The Dual Parentage/Alignment Rule**: Formally, `ZuCity` is registered under `ZuJapan`. Conceptually, `ZuCity` is the living physical core and prototype of `Chokyo`. Both relationships must be preserved without distortion.
3. **Maturity Isolation**: Never transfer the live operational reality of ZuCity onto Chokyo, Zaibots, RiChan, or JPG without explicit factual verification.
4. **Jargon Quarantine**: Never use crypto or protocol terminology (JPG/Zaibots/DeFi) in public consumer copy for ZuCity or general JGN feeds; keep technical architecture quarantined to `/network` and developer docs.

### 3.1 The Tripartite Disambiguation Protocol (JGN vs. JGC vs. JPG)
- **The Problem**: Outside observers often confuse three-letter acronyms starting with 'J'.
- **The Routing Rule**:
  - If it is public, community-facing, educational, or event-driven → Route to **JGN**.
  - If it involves voting, internal council deliberations, resource allocation, or charters → Route to **JGC** (Internal).
  - If it involves smart contracts, blockchain specifications, gas mechanics, or cryptography → Route to **JPG** (Technical).

### 3.2 The Chokyo / ZuCity / ZuJapan Triangle
- **Formal Hierarchy**: `JGN` → `ZuJapan` → `ZuCity`.
- **Narrative Framing**:
  - When communicating with corporate partners, legal entities, or diaspora societies, highlight `ZuJapan` as the institutional entity overseeing `ZuCity`.
  - When communicating with builders, nomads, and visionaries, highlight `ZuCity` as the physical prototype actively manifesting the grand vision of `Chokyo`.
  - Never present Chokyo as operational without citing ZuCity as its live proof point.

### 3.3 The Zaibots / RiChan Commercial Conversion Loop
- **Operational Relationship**: `Zaibots` provides the developer platform and economic primitives; `RiChan` is the specialized consumer application for travel and hospitality.
- **Conversion Rule**: All visitor-facing concierge inquiries on `zucity.org` convert through **Concierge Agent Credits**, which are settled across Zaibots infrastructure and routed to local Nagano merchants.

## 4. Brand Collision Case Studies: Wrong vs. Correct Execution

### Case Study 1: Promoting a Coliving Residency in Komoro
- ❌ **INCORRECT (Brand Conflation & Premature Vision Claim)**:
  > *"Come live in Chokyo, our new high-tech megacity in Nagano! Book your room on the Japan Global Council (JGC) token platform and let Zaibots autonomously manage your life."*
  - *Why it fails*: Conflates Chokyo (future vision) with live reality; confuses JGC (internal governance) with a booking platform; makes unverified claims about Zaibots.
- ✅ **CORRECT (Epistemically Grounded & Accurately Routed)**:
  > *"Experience the prototype of future city living at ZuCity in Komoro, Nagano. Stay in beautifully restored traditional homes with gigabit fiber internet, hot springs, and a world-class builder community. View rooms and upcoming residency cohorts at zucity.org/en/all."*
  - *Why it succeeds*: Accurately names ZuCity as the live physical prototype; highlights verified amenities; routes to live URL.

### Case Study 2: Explaining Network State Theory to Local Municipal Leaders
- ❌ **INCORRECT (Quarantine Violation & Alienating Jargon)**:
  > *"We are deploying a sovereign Network State in Komoro. We are using JPG smart contracts and Zaibots DeFi rails to crowdfund territory and bypass traditional municipal bureaucracy."*
  - *Why it fails*: Uses hostile, secessionist framing; dumps crypto jargon on local officials; creates regulatory and cultural panic.
- ✅ **CORRECT (Respectful Dual-Register Local Framing)**:
  > *"私共は、長野県小諸市の素晴らしい自然と歴史的景観を活かし、世界中から優秀なITクリエイターや起業家を誘致する地域創生プロジェクト「ZuCity」を推進しております。市内の空き家を伝統建築の美しさを保ちながら最新のワークスペースとして再生し、地元商店街や伝統工芸への経済貢献と持続可能な関係人口の創出を目指しています。"*
  - *Why it succeeds*: Uses respectful Japanese (*Keigo*); emphasizes regional revitalization (地方創生), *Akiya* restoration (空き家再生), and local economic partnership.

- ✅ **CORRECT (Developer-Focused & Epistemically Honest)**:

Explore our Model Context Protocol (MCP) server tools and agentic DeFi rails tested during the ETHGlobal hackathon. Build specialized skills that route real-world visitor demand to physical nodes like ZuCity. Read the developer docs at github.com/zaibots."*
  - *Why it succeeds*: Clear developer audience targeting; references verified hackathon work; maintains clear entity boundaries.
